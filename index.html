Ming Drive Uploader

A private command-line tool for its owner's Google Drive. It uploads files selected by the owner, can convert uploads to Google Docs, and can place them in an existing folder.

Its personal Gmail draft helper also creates unsent plain-text drafts with selected attachments. To, Cc and Bcc stay empty; the intended address appears only in the first line of the body. The helper has no send command.

This tool is for personal use. It is not a public service and does not offer sign-up or access to anyone else's account.

Google authorization is requested directly from Google. This informational website does not accept files, passwords, or authorization credentials.
